Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about fees, settlements, and switching.

Are there really $0 monthly POS software fees? How do you make money?
Yes, absolutely. ElitePOS operates as an integrated payment processor and POS technology provider. Instead of charging recurring monthly software subscriptions, we make a small, highly competitive margin on the card payment processing. If you don't trade, you don't pay.
When do my card takings land in my bank account?
Same day. All transactions processed up to 5:00 PM are settled to any Australian bank account by 6:00 PM that same evening, 7 days a week including weekends and public holidays.
What happens if our venue loses internet connection during service?
ElitePOS terminals feature an automated Offline Mode as well as dual-connectivity (Wi-Fi + built-in 4G cellular backup). If Wi-Fi drops, terminals failover automatically, continuing to fire kitchen dockets and take card payments without interruption.
Can I keep my existing menu, inventory items and modifiers?
Yes. Simply send us an export of your current menu from Lightspeed, Square, Kounta, or a PDF menu docket. Our hospitality onboarding team builds and organizes your menu categories and modifier shortcuts for you at zero extra charge.
Am I locked into a multi-year contract?
No lock-in contracts. We believe in earning your business every single day through superior reliability, responsive Australian support, and fair transaction rates.
